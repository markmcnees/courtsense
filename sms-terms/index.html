CourtSense Text Alerts Terms

Effective October 2026

CourtSense Alerts is the text alert program from CourtSense, LLC. It keeps you posted on the leagues and games you play in, so you know about a weather call before you drive to the courts.

What you get

League and game alerts for the programs you play in, such as:

  • Weather cancellations
  • Poll results
  • Schedule changes
  • Game-day notices

The details

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Msg and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to cancel at any time.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email hello@courtsense.app.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Who can opt in

You must be 18 or older to opt in. Consent is not a condition of joining or playing.

Turning texts on or off

Turn text alerts on or off from your CourtSense profile at any time, or reply STOP to any message.

Privacy

Your number is used only to send the alerts you asked for. Read the full Privacy Policy.
