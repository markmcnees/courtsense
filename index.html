Beach Volleyball, Organized.

CourtSense is a beach volleyball platform for the people who run the game and the people who play it. Coaches manage rosters, lineups, and live scoring for high school and college programs. League directors build schedules, track standings, and run a full season without a spreadsheet. Players find a pickup game, get a rating, and carry one profile across every court they step on.

It was built courtside in Tallahassee, Florida, by a coach and league director who needed it to work on a phone in the sand. The City of Tallahassee Parks Department runs its summer beach leagues on CourtSense, and Florida high school programs are using it for the 2026 season.
