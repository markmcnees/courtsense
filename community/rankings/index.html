How Rankings Work

Most players start at 1500. If you enter a TruVolley number on your profile, you start from a rating matched to that number instead, so your first games begin closer to your real level.

The number beside each rating is RD, or rating deviation. It shows how settled a rating is. A lower RD means the system is more confident.

New players start with a high RD because the system is still learning where they belong. The more you play, the more RD shrinks and the steadier your rating gets.

Your rating moves by a capped amount per game, so a single night will not swing it wildly. A full season is what settles it into an accurate picture.

Ratings carry across seasons and follow you between leagues and pickup. Subs do not earn rating changes.
